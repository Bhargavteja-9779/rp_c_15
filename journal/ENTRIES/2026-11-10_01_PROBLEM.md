# PROBLEM — 2026-11-10 (RECONSTRUCTED on 2026-11-24 from git history, experiments/PROTOCOL.md and result files; not written at the time.)
Growers decide whether mango is mature by comparing a handheld NIR dry-matter (DMC) reading with a specification. The calibration is trained on past seasons, and the error bar quoted with it (RMSECV) comes from those seasons. Anderson et al. (2020) documented that NIR mango DMC models lose accuracy on a new season. Nobody had measured whether the *error bars* still hold on a new season's lots, which is what a harvest decision needs.
Evidence: literature search logged in Target_Journal_Submission/REVIEW/review_02.md.

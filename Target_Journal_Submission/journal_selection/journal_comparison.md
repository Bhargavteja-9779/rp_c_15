**Reading of the landscape.** Among reputable publishers, a reported average submission-to-acceptance time
below 72 days together with a Q1 listing is rare. In this search it occurred only for Smart Agricultural
Technology; Results in Engineering missed by 2–4 days. The research topic was therefore chosen inside the
scope of Smart Agricultural Technology.

# 2026-11-24 — SURPRISE (cycle 3, plan_v4 E2) what the replay step buys
Matched baseline with the same lot scale but no m-shot replay (biascorr_full = LotCP-S without replay):
interval score equal or slightly better than LotCP-S at m ≥ 10 (diff −0.05 SNV PLS, −0.03 published PLS, CIs touch 0),
but coverage lower by 0.037/0.024/0.014/0.008 at m = 3/5/10/20 (CI excludes 0 for m ≤ 10, all seasons, both predictors),
pooled coverage 0.889 at m=3. Replay buys VALIDITY at small m, not narrower intervals. The paper's efficiency claim is
withdrawn; the contribution is restated as "m-shot replay keeps coverage at small m where plug-in bias correction with
the same scale under-covers". With replicate scale only (biascorr_rep), LotCP-S is better on both coverage and score.

# RESULT — 2026-11-24 (written at the time) — G6 replay ablation (plan_v2 E2; results/ablation_cycle2/replay_ablation.csv)
LotCP-S without the episodic replay (each past lot standardised with its own full-information bias and scale) under-covers at small m: coverage 0.757 (m = 0), 0.888 (m = 3), 0.900 (m = 5) vs 0.894, 0.920, 0.920 with replay; share of lots reaching 0.90: 0.36/0.56/0.60 vs 0.77/0.71/0.70. Interval score is equal or slightly better without replay (m = 10: 4.38 vs 4.44) because the intervals are narrower. Conclusion: the replay contributes validity (coverage at the nominal level when the bias and scale are estimated from few samples), not efficiency. Lot-balanced weighting made little difference on mango (lot sizes homogeneous); it is kept because the guarantee requires it.

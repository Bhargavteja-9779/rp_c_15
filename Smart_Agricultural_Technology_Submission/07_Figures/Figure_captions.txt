Fig. 1. Risk-aware harvest workflow. Before the season, past lots are used to calibrate the few-shot correction and to plan the number of reference fruit. In the orchard, fruit of a new lot are scanned, m of them are tested destructively, and each fruit receives a calibrated lower bound that is compared with the dry-matter specification.

Fig. 2. Why calibration on held-out samples fails (SNV PLS, forward chaining). (a) Residuals of sample-fold cross-validation in the source seasons and residuals on the following season. (b) Mean residual of each target lot; the bar marks the season mean.

Fig. 3. Lot-macro coverage, mean width and interval score against the number of reference fruit per lot (SNV PLS, forward chaining, 137 target lots, nominal 0.90). Dashed lines: split conformal prediction without reference fruit.

Fig. 4. Lot-level assurance (SNV PLS, forward chaining). (a) Share of lot-draws in which at least 90 % of the fruit is covered; dotted lines mark the targets of LotCP-A for δ = 0.10 and δ = 0.20. (b) Mean interval width.

Fig. 5. Trade-off between harvesting immature fruit and holding back mature fruit (SNV PLS, specification 15 % DMC, 137 later-season lots; exploratory). Each curve traces one rule as its level α varies from 0.02 to 0.5; crosses are rules without a level. (a) Fruit-level decision. (b) Lot-level decision: a lot is harvested when at least 80 % of its scanned fruit are accepted.

Fig. 6. Reference-budget planning. (a) Width planned from past lots against the mean width realised on the following season; each point is one target season, predictor, number of reference fruit and variant. (b) Planned and realised curves for SNV PLS, averaged over target seasons.

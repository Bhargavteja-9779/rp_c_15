## Addendum (same day): definition check and sensitivity
While writing a Monte-Carlo test of the season-level statement, every simulated season quantile was +inf (8 lots per
season < 9 needed for a finite conformal quantile at α = 0.10) and the test passed trivially. I first switched
group_quantiles to the PLAIN empirical quantile; the real-data gain then disappeared (multipliers 1.00–1.07). On review,
the pre-specified definition (plan_v7: the per-season replay quantile as LotCP computes it, i.e. the CONFORMAL quantile for
a new lot of that season) is valid for Proposition 3 and is the primary analysis; it was restored. The plain variant is
reported as a sensitivity analysis: it removes the marginal gain (seasons ≥ 0.90: 3–4/5) but keeps most of the lot-level
gain (ARplain: lot target met in 3–5/5 seasons at +2–6 % width). Mechanism: within-season finite-sample calibration of
few-lot seasons + an upper order statistic over seasons; neither alone suffices for marginal coverage.
Simulation (12 lots/season, exchangeable seasons): hit rates 0.74 (600 reps; bound 0.75), 0.833 (bound 0.833),
0.713 (bound 0.667).

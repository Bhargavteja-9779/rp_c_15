## Requirements from the brief that could not be fully met or verified

* **≤ 72 days to final acceptance** — met on the publisher-reported *average* (69–72 days), with no margin. Individual manuscripts can take longer; a major revision would likely exceed it.
* **≈ 30 days to acceptance** — not met. No legitimate Q1 journal with a reported average near 30 days was found.
* **Q1 / Impact Factor inside Clarivate JCR** — taken from the publisher page and JCR-derived listings, not from JCR itself. The journal sits in the ESCI edition, not SCIE; some institutions count only SCIE journals. If that matters, verify before submitting.
* **"Oxford-type" publisher** — Oxford University Press journals that fit the topic were examined and rejected on timeline (see `journal_comparison.md`): none came close to 72 days.

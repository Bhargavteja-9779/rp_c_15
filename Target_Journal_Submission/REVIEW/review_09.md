# Review round 9 — Associate Editor, Smart Agricultural Technology

Question: would this manuscript survive peer review at this journal?

**Scope.** Within scope (sensing instrument used for an agricultural production decision; data-driven method;
practical recommendation). The paper is more statistical than the journal's typical article; the introduction and
the "practical procedure" paragraph keep the agronomic use in view.

**Novelty.** Applied novelty: first lot-level coverage assessment across harvest seasons for fruit NIR, with a
concrete reference-sampling recommendation. Methodological novelty is modest and stated as such.

**Technical quality and evidence.** Forward-chaining design, grouped folds, pre-specified protocol with listed
deviations, three predictors, ablation with negative results reported, paired tests, robustness analyses.
Stronger than typical submissions on these points.

**Remaining concerns an editor would raise**

| # | Severity | Concern | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9.1 | MAJOR | Single data set. Reviewers will ask for a second fruit or attribute. | Open. Cannot be fixed with public data; stated as the first limitation. |
| 9.2 | MAJOR | No field validation of the procedure by the authors; the "reference fruit" protocol is simulated from archived data. | Open; inherent to a secondary-data study. Stated ("Reference sampling" limitation). |

Elsevier requires disclosure, human oversight and full author responsibility, and does not allow AI to be listed as an author. | 9.6 | MODERATE | Length. | See round 7 (7.12). |

**Editor's forecast.** Likely sent to review. Most probable outcome of a fair review: major revision asking for a
second data set or a clearer statement of scope. A decision within the journal's average 69–72 days would require
at most one revision round.

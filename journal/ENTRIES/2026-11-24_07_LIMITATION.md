# LIMITATION — 2026-11-24 (written at the time)
The replay needs enough past lots with at least m + 10 samples. In NeoSpectra at m = 20 there are about 8 such lots per source fold; the lot-balanced quantile then sits near the largest score and the intervals become very wide (≈ 2× the baselines). The planner shows this in advance (planned width), but the paper must state a minimum number of past lots (cycle 1 robustness analysis: about 40) for the method to be efficient.
